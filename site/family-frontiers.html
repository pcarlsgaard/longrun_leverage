Assumptions and strategy definitions

The full comparison contains 300 base-price portfolios. The 44 S&P 500 and Nasdaq call designs have three annual-roll reserve variants and a range of dip rules. Additional dip variants were tested on the six principal regimes. Annual rolls buy calls near two years to expiry and replace them with about one year remaining. For the dip strategies the reserve is initially half long Treasuries and half three-month bills. In 90/40, 90 is the strike as a percentage of the underlying at purchase and 40 is the percentage of portfolio value budgeted for calls at the annual roll. The staged rule can buy up to 5% of NAV after each of the 20% and 30% Nasdaq drawdowns, executed at the following close and capped at the normal call allocation and a 10% reserve for purchases per annual roll. The two triggers reset after recovery to within 5% of the rolling 252-session closing high. S&P own-index variants instead use S&P drawdowns.

The matched-reserve comparison uses 44 annual and 44 staged portfolios and allows a surcharge on extra calls. In the focused 90/40 view the matched annual control uses a 40.61% premium budget. Results are nominal, pretax, and permit fractional positions. Historical option marks use modeled implied volatility and 1% friction on each side.

Rotations use the S&P’s 185- or 200-day price moving average, switch with a one-trading-day delay and pay 0.25% per switch. They hold SSO, UPRO or TQQQ above the average and either 1× equities or bills below it. Fixed leveraged portfolios hold individual funds or quarterly-rebalanced stock/bond mixes. The unlevered portfolios are the Nasdaq-100, S&P 500, and 60% S&P 500 with 40% long Treasuries.

Simulations draw joint market blocks with replacement. Each strategy is compared on the same paths for each block length. The simulated coordinates are the separate medians of annualized return and maximum drawdown. Frontier membership is descriptive and can shift for close points. A point qualifies when no included alternative in its comparison has at least as much growth and no greater loss with one strict improvement. Connecting lines are visual guides between tested choices.
